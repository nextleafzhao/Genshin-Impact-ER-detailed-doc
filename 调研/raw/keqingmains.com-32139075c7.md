# Odette Quick Guide

###### *Updated for Version 7.0*

Odette is a 5-star Cryo Sword character in Genshin Impact who provides off-field Cryo damage while enabling and buffing Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl reactions. Learn about Odette’s best builds, weapons, artifacts, and teams in this quick guide.

**Note that the information given may change if new discoveries are made.** More extensive testing is in progress.

# Character Overview

## Pros

* Enables and buffs both Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl reactions.
* Provides consistent, wide-AoE off-field Cryo application and damage via her Elemental Skill.
* Highly accessible F2P weapons and easy to build.
* Beautiful dance animations and swan motifs.

## Cons

* Loses significant value outside of Stellar teams.

## Playstyles

#### **Off-Field Stellar Glimmer Support**

Odette is primarily played as an off-field support in Stellar Glimmer teams. She casts her Skill twice to deal Stellar Glimmer damage and to summon a Solo Dance Double. While off-field, she provides consistent Cryo application, damage, and buffing. Due to its long animation, Odette’s Elemental Burst is rarely used despite its personal buffs, being secondary to maintaining Skill uptime and the Solo Dance Double’s damage.

## Talents

### Level and Talent Priority

Skill >> Level 90 ≥ Burst

Odette’s Elemental Skill is her main source of off-field Cryo damage and application, making it her most critical Talent to level. Character Level 90 provides a minor base stat increase, which assists in reaching her ATK threshold. Her Elemental Burst provides a minor personal DMG bonus and refreshes her Skill duration, but contributes a relatively low proportion of her total team value and is often skipped in rotations. Her Normal Attack Talent can be left completely unleveled as she operates exclusively off-field.

##### **Normal Attack** | *Snow Swan Variation*

Talent Description

***Normal Attack**  
Unleashes a sword attack of up to 5 consecutive strikes.*

***Charged Attack**  
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash a dazzling slash on opponents in front of her.*

***Plunging Attack**  
Attacks opponents in her path while plunging from mid-air, then deals AoE DMG upon landing.*

Odette’s Normal Attacks contribute virtually nothing to her gameplay and should almost never be used. Since all of her damage and utility comes from her other Talents, this Talent can remain unleveled.

Elemental Skill

##### **Elemental Skill** | *Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers*

Talent Description

*With slow, graceful dance steps, Odette deals **AoE Cryo DMG** to the opponent, and also summons her Solo Dance Double to the field.  
If a Dance Double summoned by Odette is already on the field, this will re-summon the Dance Double and reset its duration.*

***Solo Dance Double**  
Alternates between the Plume and Wing dance moves, periodically attacking nearby opponents and dealing to them **AoE Cryo DMG**.  
Additionally, for 6s after unleashing the Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers, Odette’s Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers will become the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn’s Tolling instead, where a dance duet deals **AoE Cryo DMG** to nearby opponents over time. Then, when the duet ends, she deals another instance of **AoE Cryo DMG** that is considered Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl DMG.*

Odette’s Elemental Skill serves as the foundation of her kit. Activating it deals initial AoE Cryo damage and summons a Solo Dance Double, which persists for 20s. While Odette is off-field, this summon rotates between Plume and Wing dance sequences to provide sustained AoE Cryo damage and trigger Stellar Glimmer reactions.

After the initial activation, Odette can execute Adagio: Coda at Dawn’s Tolling. This follow-up Skill initiates a dance duet that concludes with a strong burst of Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl damage depending on her  current Radiance state. The Solo Dance Double remains active throughout, with its standard attacks becoming empowered to deal bonus damage based on the active Stellar reaction.

The enhanced Skill has a 15-second cooldown and cannot be reset by her Elemental Burst.

Elemental Burst

##### **Elemental Burst** | *Presto: Bluebird Finale*

Talent Description

*With quick, lively dance steps, Odette deals multiple instances of **AoE Cryo DMG**, and summons her Solo Dance Double. She also gains Snow Swan’s Dream, which increases the Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG Odette deals.  
Additionally, for 6s after unleashing the Elemental Burst Presto: Bluebird Finale, Odette’s Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers will be replaced with the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn’s Tolling instead.*

*If there is a Solo Dance Double summoned by Odette on the field, it will be summoned to her side with its duration refreshed.*

Odette’s Burst deals multiple instances of Cryo damage while refreshing or summoning her Solo Dance Double, ensuring uninterrupted Skill uptime. It also grants Snow Swan’s Dream, increasing Odette’s Stellar Glimmer reaction damage for 20s, and immediately grants access to Adagio: Coda at Dawn’s Tolling.

Although the Burst buffs Odette, its long animation makes it unwieldy to use, and the damage dealt is rather underwhelming. As a result, the Burst is not worth building ER for and often skipped, even if available.

Ascension 1 Passive

##### **Ascension 1 Passive** | *Spring Rite of the Chosen One*

Talent Description

*When Odette summons her Solo Dance Double, she also obtains 4 stacks of Marvelous Splendor.*

Summoning her Solo Dance Double grants 4 stacks of Marvelous Splendor, each increasing Stellar Glimmer DMG by 15%. After Odette leaves the field, these stacks are gradually transferred to nearby party members, allowing her to provide up to a 60% Stellar Glimmer DMG Bonus while occupying minimal field time.

Ascension 4 Passive

##### **Ascension 4 Passive** | *Pathetique of Pateticheskaya*

Talent Description

*For every 100 ATK Odette has over 1,000, her Stellar Glimmer DMG is additionally increased by 1.5% of the original DMG. She can deal up to 30% more additional DMG in this way.*

Odette Stellar Glimmer damage is increased based on her ATK, receiving an additional 1.5% of the reaction’s original damage for every 100 ATK above 1000, up to a maximum of 30% at 3000 ATK. This is a unique multiplier for her own direct Stellar damage and makes ATK even more useful for her until she reaches the cap.

Stellar Jubilee Passive

##### **Stellar Jubilee Passive** | *Dance of Aurore*

Talent Description

*Odette will enter the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state when she is inside a Polestar Field, or the Radiance: Stellar Swirl state for 8s after a nearby party member triggers a Stellar Swirl reaction.  
When a party member triggers a Superconduct or Cryo Swirl reaction, it becomes a Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl reaction instead, and the Base DMG of said reaction is also increased by 0.7% for every 100 points of Odette’s ATK. A maximum increase of 14% can be obtained in this way.*

Converts Superconduct and Cryo Swirl into Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl, respectively, while also increasing the Base DMG of these reactions based on Odette’s ATK. If both reactions are triggered, Odette will prioritize entering the Stellar-Conduct state.

### C1 vs R1

While R1 is a decent increase in damage for Odette, her contribution of personal damage to the team is low enough to massively incentivize getting her C1 (which further buffs the team) over her R1, especially since her craftable option is not far behind her signature weapon for team performance. Getting C1 also brings Odette closer to her very potent C2.

## Mechanics

#### **Stellar-Conduct**

Stellar-Conduct is triggered by applying Cryo and Electro to an opponent. Rather than dealing reaction damage directly, it creates a Polestar Field, which grants party-wide buffs, reduces enemies’ Physical RES, and enables certain characters to enter the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state. While the field is active, additional Cryo and Electro application strengthens its buffs. Direct Stellar-Conduct damage can only be dealt through certain character abilities.

#### ****Stellar Swirl****

Stellar Swirl is triggered by applying Anemo to an opponent affected by Cryo. It creates a StellarVortex that deals Stellar Swirl Anemo damage upon creation before exploding after a short delay to deal AoE Stellar Swirl Cryo damage. Repeated Stellar Swirl triggers strengthen the Vortex and increase its explosion damage, while also granting applicable characters the Radiance: Stellar Swirl state to enhance their Talents.

For further information , check out the [Stellar Glimmer Reaction Guide.](https://keqingmains.com/misc/stellar/)

**Stat Priority:** CRIT > ATK% > EM

Odette’s main source of damage comes from her direct Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl damage, which scales with ATK, CRIT, and EM. She values ATK more due to her A4 passive, so ATK% should be prioritized over EM. Cryo DMG Bonus does not affect Stellar Glimmer damage, so ATK% Goblet is preferred. However, building CRIT is the priority.

## Stellar-Conduct

![](https://kqm-uploads.keqingmains.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Portraits-Odette-Yae-MikoElectro-CryoElectro-Fill-slot.webp)

****Odette — Yae Miko / Electro — Cryo/Electro — Flex****

Odette’s main role in Stellar-Conduct teams is to provide strong off-field Cryo damage and teamwide Stellar damage buffs. An Electro teammate (such as Yae Miko, Alyosha, or Beidou) is required to trigger Stellar-Conduct and establish the Polestar Field. The team also includes an on-field carry (such as Sandrone, Wriothesley, or Cryo Traveler) as the primary damage dealer. The flex slot is typically filled by a sustain unit, an additional Electro or Cryo off-field DPS, or a secondary buffer.

Nicole offers straightforward buffing and comfort, Xilonen provides a large amount of RES shred, while C6 Beidou brings RES shred, EM buffs and extra Electro application. Wriothesley does not prefer healers due to him needing to be below 60% HP to reliably trigger his enhanced CA, which does direct Stellar-Conduct damage.

## Stellar Swirl

![](https://kqm-uploads.keqingmains.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Portraits-Odette-Yumemizuki-MizukiAnemo-Cryo-Fill-slot.webp)

******Odette — Yumemizuki Mizuki/Anemo — Cryo — Flex******

Odette’s role in Stellar Swirl teams is to provide consistent off-field Cryo application while enabling strong Stellar Swirl uptime for an Anemo on-field carry. The Anemo teammate is responsible for triggering Stellar Swirl and driving the team’s damage, while the second Cryo unit helps maintain Cryo aura and adds extra damage or utility. The flex slot usually goes to sustain, additional buffs, or another off-field damage dealer.
