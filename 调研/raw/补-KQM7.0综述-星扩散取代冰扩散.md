〔逐字抄录 Stellar Glimmer Reactions 章〕

Stellar Glimmer Reactions are the enhanced versions of conventional reactions, brought up by characters from Snezhnaya (Sandrone, Odette and Cryo Traveler as of 7.0). These Stellar-Glimmer Reactions can be triggered by any teammates as long as there is a Snezhnaya unit that enables the reactions in the team. Each Stellar Glimmer Reaction behaves differently from the others and **from their conventional counterpart**.

## Stellar Conduct

Stellar-Conduct (SSC) is the special **Electro–Cryo** reaction that **replaces Superconduct (SC)** when certain Snezhnaya characters or Cryo Traveler are in the team.

## Stellar Swirl

Stellar Swirl (SSW) is the special **Anemo–Cryo** reaction that **replaces the normal Cryo Swirl reaction** when certain Snezhnaya characters (Sandrone, Odette, and Cryo Traveler as of 7.0) are in the team. When SSW is triggered, a Stellar Vortex will be generated on the field. After a fixed time, this Vortex will explode to deal Cryo SSW damage.

〔逐字抄录 Scarlet Proof（血红之证）4件套 说明，涉及触发者归属〕

* The effect can **only** be triggered if the wearer is an Anemo character, as **triggering SSW only counts as applying Anemo on existing Cryo aura**. Cryo characters dealing direct SSW damage from their Talents cannot trigger the set.

（核实要点——逐字关键句）

1. **「the special Anemo–Cryo reaction that replaces the normal Cryo Swirl reaction」**
   → 被取代者精确到「**the normal Cryo Swirl reaction**」（普通**冰**扩散）。KQM 在同一章对星超导写的是「replaces Superconduct」，两者句式对称：一个取代超导、一个取代**冰扩散**，**未涉及水/火/雷扩散**。

2. **「from the normal Cryo Swirl reaction」**（New Content 表）
   → 再次限定来源为「normal **Cryo** Swirl」。

3. **「triggering SSW only counts as applying Anemo on existing Cryo aura」**
   → 触发判定 = 「向**已有冰附着**的目标施加**风元素**」。措辞用的是 **Cryo aura**（冰附着），不是「any aura」；若非冰附着亦可触发，此处不可能写成 Cryo aura 而不作说明。

4. 该章只出现 Electro–Cryo（星超导）与 Anemo–Cryo（星扩散）两组元素配对，**没有**第三组配对。
