## Introduction

Stellar-Conduct is the first Stellar Reaction introduced in Genshin. Coming out alongside Sandrone and the Witch's Revelation buffs in Patch 6.7, it is a meta-defining Reaction with many intricacies and several powerful characters that can make use of it. Stellar-Conduct and [Stellar Swirl](https://mobalytics.gg/genshin-impact/guides/stellar-swirl) are presently the only Stellar Reactions, and are going to be very important for the meta and endgame of Snezhnaya moving forward.

## Stellar-Conduct Reaction Explanation

### What Is Stellar-Conduct?

Stellar-Conduct is the modified version of the Superconduct reaction. Superconduct is the Reaction that occurs when Cryo is applied on an enemy with Electro on them, or vice versa.

If a character that enables Stellar-Conduct is in the current party, when Superconduct would trigger, Stellar-Conduct will trigger instead. The characters that can do this are, as of 7.0:

There are many more characters that either significantly interact with Stellar-Conduct in their kit or outright require it to become meta viable, but cannot inherently enable Stellar-Conduct.

### What Does It Do?

Regular Superconduct deals a bit of damage that scales on the Elemental Mastery of the character that triggered it, and reduces the Physical RES of enemies hit by it by 40% for 20 seconds. Stellar-Conduct's effects are entirely different. When a Stellar-Conduct Reaction is triggered, it will initially do two things:

*   Spawn a 'Polestar Field' with a duration of 6 seconds, or refresh its duration if one already exists.
*   Trigger "Radiance: Stellar-Conduct" for 8 seconds.

The Polestar Field contains the core mechanics of the reaction, whereas the Stellar Radiance is what enables the Stellar-Conduict parts of Stellar characters.

### The Polestar Field

After it spawns, the Polestar Field will begin 'recording' all Cryo and Electro Application dealt to enemies within its range. This recording phase will last for 4 seconds, and when it ends, it will:

*   Provide a buff to the Stellar-Conduct, Cryo and Electro damage of party members within the Field
*   End the current recording and begin a new one

The buff it provides scales with the amount of Cryo and Electro Application it recorded, with each counter going from 0 to 12.

*   Each Cryo or Electro Application within its recording period will count for 1 such stack. 
*   Cryo or Electro damage that doesn't apply the corresponding Element **will not** provide a stack to the Polestar Field.
