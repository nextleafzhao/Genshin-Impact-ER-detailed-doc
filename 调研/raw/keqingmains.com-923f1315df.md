# Stellar Glimmer Reactions

Stellar Glimmer Reactions are the enhanced versions of conventional reactions, brought up by characters from Snezhnaya (Sandrone, Odette and Cryo Traveler as of 7.0). These Stellar-Glimmer Reactions can be triggered by any teammates as long as there is a Snezhnaya unit that enables the reactions in the team. Each Stellar Glimmer Reaction behaves differently from the others and from their conventional counterpart. Snezhnaya characters and some characters from other regions also have methods to deal Stellar-Glimmer Reaction damage directly from their kits.

## Stellar Conduct

Stellar-Conduct (SSC) is the special Electro–Cryo reaction that replaces Superconduct (SC) when certain Snezhnaya characters or Cryo Traveler are in the team. SSC was already introduced in Version “Luna VIII” via the release of Sandrone. As of Version 7.0, there are three characters that can enable SSC, namely Sandrone, Odette and Cryo Traveler.

![](https://kqm-uploads.keqingmains.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Stellar-Conduct-Reaction.png)

A Polestar Field is summoned when Stellar-Conduct is triggered.

Further information about SSC mechanics can be found in this [Stellar Glimmer Reaction Guide](http://kqm.gg/misc/stellar/#Stellar-Conduct).

## Stellar Swirl

Stellar Swirl (SSW) is the special Anemo–Cryo reaction that replaces the normal Cryo Swirl reaction when certain Snezhnaya characters (Sandrone, Odette, and Cryo Traveler as of 7.0) are in the team. When SSW is triggered, a Stellar Vortex will be generated on the field. After a fixed time, this Vortex will explode to deal Cryo SSW damage.

![](https://kqm-uploads.keqingmains.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Stellar-Swirl-Reaction.png)

When Stellar Swirl is triggered, a Stellar Vortex will be generated.

Further information about SSW mechanics can be found in this [Stellar Glimmer Reaction Guide](http://kqm.gg/misc/stellar/#Stellar_Swirl).

# New Artifact Sets Overview

The new artifact sets are found in the Domain of Blessing “Inverted Glacier”, located on the Everfrozen Earth, northwest of the railway between Rankova Zorya and the Central Station.

Snezhnaya’s two new artifact sets revolve around Stellar Glimmer Reactions.

DISCLAIMER: The following suggestions are based on early analysis. Please wait for further calculations and testing before investing a significant amount of Resin into farming these artifacts.

## Scarlet Proof

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **2-Piece Set Bonus** | ATK +18%. |
| **4-Piece Set Bonus** | Increases the equipping character’s CRIT Rate by 16%, and their Stellar Swirl reaction dealt by 40%, for 10s after they trigger a Stellar Swirl reaction. |

The 2pc effect of Scarlet Proof (SP) provides a straightforward 18% ATK buff, while the 4pc further gives 16% CRIT Rate and 40% SSW DMG Bonus to the equipping character after they trigger Stellar-Swirl. These buffs apply to both the reaction damage (from triggering the reaction) and Direct SSW damage (from characters’ Talents). This makes the set a good option for SSW damage dealers. However, there are two caveats to this set:

* The effect can **only** be triggered if the wearer is an Anemo character, as triggering SSW only counts as applying Anemo on existing Cryo aura. Cryo characters dealing direct SSW damage from their Talents cannot trigger the set.
* The effect can only be triggered while **on-field**. However, the buffs last for 10s on the wearer even if they leave the field afterwards.

### Which Characters Can Use Scarlet Proof?

4pc SP can be used on any Anemo units who trigger a fair amount of SSW or deal Direct SSW damage. However, there are competing options to run on Anemo units, such as 4pc Viridescent Venerer, or 4pc Instructor. As such, as of the time of writing, 4pc SP is not the best set for any character.

|  |  |

### **Which Characters Can Use Heart of the Furnace?**

Any SSC or SSW team should have someone hold the set for its teamwide Stellar Glimmer DMG% buffs. However, it is often reserved for support characters to let teammates with higher damage contribution use a higher offensive set.

# New Battle-Pass Weapon Overview

The new set of Battle Pass weapons are CRIT Rate stat sticks (except the Polearm), each of which having a somewhat unique buffing mechanic. Of the new selection, the Bow and Catalyst stand out as potent 4-star options, but are by no means required on characters they work well on.

DISCLAIMER: The following suggestions are based on early analysis. Please wait for further calculations and testing before purchasing and selecting a Battle Pass weapon.

## Heretic’s Molten Blade (Sword)

|  |
| --- |
| **Base ATK:** 510 **CRIT Rate:** 27.6%  When the equipping character unleashes their Elemental Skill, they gain “Gleam of First Light”: Every second, increases said character’s ATK by between 18–36% and 36–72% depending on how much they have traveled over the last 1s while the effect is active. This effect, which lasts 14s, can trigger once every 14s. It is removed when the equipping character leaves the field. |

Heretic’s Molten Blade tracks the movement of its holder and gives a corresponding ATK% buff value based on how much they have traveled in a 1-second period. This buff refreshes every second and is removed when the wearer switches off-field. By default, the holder gains 18% ATK (at R1) from just standing still, and the more they travel around, the bigger the buff becomes. Typically, a 1-second continuous dash can cover enough distance to give the full ATK% buff, with running and jumping resulting in less distance. Overall, this is a fairly good 4-star weapon for on-field characters who move around frequently or characters whose abilities can snapshot the higher ATK% value. However, R1 Heretic’s Molten Blade performs only similarly to F2P 4-star options such as Finale of the Deep or Calamity of Eshu for most characters it can work on. This makes it not recommended to be selected from the Battle’s Pass in general as of now.

| --- | --- |

## Forged by the Golden Melody (Claymore)

|  |
| --- |
| **Base ATK:** 510 **CRIT Rate:** 27.6%  Every 10s, the equipping character plays a “Harmonic Movement” of the corresponding type for a boost in the following order: +18–36% ATK > +120–240 Elemental Mastery > +28–56% Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG. Each instance of Harmonic Movement lasts 10s. This effect can trigger even when the equipping character is not on the field.  Triggering a Stellar Glimmer reaction will also grant an additional 12-second instance of “Harmonic Movement: Contrapuntal” with the same effects as the Harmonic Movement active when Stellar Glimmer is triggered. This effect stacks with the original Harmonic Movement effect, and can trigger once every 12s. |

Forged by the Golden Melody’s passive can be broken down into two parts. First, its holder will gain a buff (“Harmonic Movement”) that lasts 10s. This buff starts as ATK% when the fight begins. After 10s it will change into EM, and Stellar Glimmer DMG% after another 10s, before cycling back to ATK%. This happens even if the wearer stays off-field.  
  
Triggering a Stellar Glimmer reaction activates the second buff, “Harmonic Movement: Contrapuntal.” It stacks with the base “Harmonic Movement” buff and provides identical stat values, but runs on its own independent duration. Consequently, the base “Harmonic Movement” can transition to its next phase while “Contrapuntal” remains active.

In general, this weapon is valued by Stellar Glimmer DPS units such as Sandrone. However, she can struggle to trigger the “Contrapuntal” effect in many of her best teams where Electro aura uptime is low. Apart from Sandrone, this weapon remains a decent stat stick that performs worse than Serpent Spine for most Claymore users as they cannot utilize the Stellar Glimmer DMG Bonus and trigger the second half of the passive.

#### Best Users

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **[Sandrone](https://keqingmains.com/q/sandrone-quickguide/)** | Forged by the Golden Melody is Sandrone’s best 4-star weapon, especially at high refinements, in teams where there is enough Electro application to establish an Electro aura right at the start of her field time for her to react with. At R5, it can compete with Sandrone’s top weapons such as Thousands Blazing Suns.  However, without the “Contrapuntal” effect due to the lack of Electro aura uptime, this Claymore at high refinements only performs a bit better than her other 4-star options. |

## Frostbreath (Polearm)

|  |
| --- |
| **Base ATK:** 510 **ER:** 45.9%  Triggering a Cryo or Hydro-related elemental reaction increases the equipping character’s ATK by 20–40% for the next 15s, as well as regenerates 6–12 Elemental Energy for other members of their party. This effect can trigger once every 16s. |

Frostbreath is an ER weapon that gives Flat Energy to all teammates (except the holder) when a Cryo or Hydro reaction is triggered. This weapon can be compared with Favonius Lance and with its own pros and cons. When to consider Frostbreath over Favonius Lance:

* The wielder can trigger a Cryo or Hydro reaction while on-field.
* High refinements on Frostbreath.
* Building CRIT Rate for Favonius wastes the stat opportunity that should go toward buffing.
* Fighting against shielded enemies that prevent Favonius from being triggered.
* Favonius passive can only be triggered once every rotation.
* Teammates have less than 167% ER initially with R1 Frostbreath, or less than 333% with R5 Frostbreath (one Favonius proc regenerates more Energy for off-field characters with higher ER than this threshold).
* The wielder does not need the extra Energy from self Favonius proc.

However, unless players do not need other BP weapons, it is not strongly recommended to get this weapon to replace Favonius Lance.

| **[Shenhe](https://keqingmains.com/q/shenhe-quickguide/)** | With this Polarm, Shenhe does not need to build CRIT Rate to trigger Favonius. She can instead reallocate her artifact substats to more ATK% and ER rolls. However, Favonius Lance is still recommended in quickswap teams where Shenhe can get 2 Favonius procs each rotation. |

## Clash of Kings (Catalyst)

|  |
| --- |
| **Base ATK:** 510 **CRIT Rate:** 27.6%  Using an Elemental Skill grants the equipping character “Laws of the Board,” which increases their ATK by 20–40% and their Elemental Mastery by 100–200. This effect lasts 6s and can trigger once every 12s. Does not stack. The duration of this effect will also be extended by 6s if the equipping character hits an opponent with a Charged Attack while it is active. The effect can be extended for max 6s in this way. |

This Catalyst provides simple but effective ATK% and EM buff after using an Elemental Skill, and extending the buff duration by 6s after using a Charged Attack. It best suits on-field ATK-scaling reaction damage dealers, or off-field ones who can swap back in to refresh the weapon’s passive.

## Jade Vista (Bow)

|  |
| --- |
| **Base ATK:** 510 **CRIT Rate:** 27.6%  For every party member other than the equipping character: – Who is of the same Elemental Type as the equipper: The equipping character’s Elemental Mastery is increased by 64–128; – Who is not of the same Elemental Type as the equipper: The equipping character’s ATK increases by 12–24%.  The two effects described above can stack up to 3 times in total, with Elemental Mastery buffs applied first. |

Unlike the previous BP Bow, Jade Vista is a more versatile weapon. Its passive gives the wielder EM and ATK% buffs depending on the numbers of teammates with similar and different Elements to the wearer. Due to its passive mechanics, this Bow should be avoided on characters dealing raw damage in a team full of same Element teammates.  
  
Jade Vista ranks among the best 4-star options for the characters below, often serving as their top 4-star choice at R1 and rivaling 5-star bows at higher refinements. This is thanks to the sheer amount of stats it provides that are easy to utilize.  
  
However, the Bow is only a slight increase or a sidegrade to existing other 4-star options, and for most wearers who are not the primary damage dealers in the team, its refinements only result in a slight total DPS increase.

With refinements, Jade Vista competes with many 5-star Bows. |

The only exception is Freeze teams where the Bow’s CRIT Rate makes it easy to overcap with 4pc BS or 4pc MH, thus losing to Range Gauge. |

Jade Vista is not recommended in Freeze/Mono Cryo teams due to CRIT Rate overcapping and useless EM buffs. |

Additionally, the Traveler’s ATK will also increase by 16-32% for **8**s, and regenerate **5** Elemental Energy, after they attack and hit an opponent. This effect can trigger once every **5**s. This can be triggered even when the character is not on the field. |
