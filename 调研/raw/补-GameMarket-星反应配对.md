## What Stellar reactions are

Stellar reactions belong to the **Stellar-Glimmer** family introduced with Version 7.0. There are two variants, and they require different elemental pairings:

-   **Stellar-Conduct** — needs **Cryo and Electro**
-   **Stellar-Swirl** — needs **Anemo** alongside Cryo

Odette, the 5-star Cryo sword support launching with the version, can trigger both, which is why she's positioned as the system's premier enabler.

（中略：Polestar Field 效果、ATK-over-EM 论述）

## Stellar-Swirl and its constraint

Stellar-Swirl is the Anemo-based variant, and it carries a practical limitation worth knowing before you build around it: **no Anemo character currently carries the Stellar mechanic natively.**

That means in Stellar-Swirl teams, your Cryo enabler has to carry the reaction herself — in practice, Odette, usually alongside Sandrone.
