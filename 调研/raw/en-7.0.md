"Everwinter Without Mercy" Version 7.0 Update Details
Date Posted Aug 12, 2026

Dear Traveler,

Below are the details of the Version 7.0 update "Everwinter Without Mercy" and the update compensation.

〓Compensation Details〓

Maintenance Compensation: Primogems ×300 (60 Primogems per hour the servers are down)

Issue Fix Compensation: Primogems ×300 (please refer to the relevant compensation mail for more details)

〓Scope of Compensation〓

Maintenance Compensation: Travelers who have reached Adventure Rank 5 or above by 2026/08/12 06:00 (UTC+8).

Compensation must be claimed before the end of Version 7.0.

Issue Fix Compensation: Travelers who have reached Adventure Rank 5 or above by 2026/08/12 06:00 (UTC+8).

Please log in and claim your compensation before 2026/08/15 06:00 (UTC+8).

Our developers will distribute compensation to Travelers via in-game mail within 5 hours after the update maintenance is finished. The mail will expire after 30 days, so don't forget to claim the attached compensation in time.

〓Update Schedule〓

Update maintenance begins 2026/08/12 06:00 (UTC+8) and is estimated to take 5 hours.

〓How to Update Game Client〓

PC: Close the game, open the Genshin Impact Launcher, and click Update.

iOS: Open the App Store and tap Update.

Android: Open the game and follow the directions on-screen.

PlayStation®: Highlight "Genshin Impact" from the Home screen, press the OPTIONS button and select "Check for Update."

Xbox Series X|S: Open the system main menu, select "My games & apps," select the "Manage" tab, and on the "Updates" page, find "Genshin Impact" and confirm to update.

Please do not hesitate to contact Customer Service if you encounter any issues installing the new version. We will do our very best to resolve the issue.

V. Elemental Reaction

New Stellar Glimmer Reaction: Stellar Swirl

Under certain conditions, Anemo and Cryo can trigger Stellar Swirl, dealing one instance of Anemo DMG to the target and creating a Stellar Vortex. Only one Stellar Vortex can exist within the same area at a time.

A Stellar Vortex detonates after a short delay, dealing AoE Cryo DMG. Triggering Stellar Swirl nearby a certain number of times increases the vortex's level, expanding its blast radius and increasing its DMG. Triggering additional Stellar Swirl reactions nearby can also cause the vortex to detonate early. The aforementioned DMG can trigger CRIT Hits.

When the Stellar Vortex explodes, it will also grant nearby characters the ability to jump higher once, for a short time.

Additionally, triggering Stellar Swirl grants certain nearby characters the "Radiance: Stellar Swirl" effect, enhancing some of their Talents and skill effects.

Certain characters can also utilize their Talents to deal Stellar Swirl DMG to enemies. This DMG can trigger CRIT Hits.

VI. New Enemies

Immortal Construct

◇ A nigh-immortal life form created through bio-alchemy.

The Immortal Construct burns its life to enter the "Lifeflare" state, in which it gains several HP shields at one time. Any DMG dealt by it while in this state will restore its HP. When the number of HP-restoring shields goes below a certain number or after Lifeflare lasts for a set time, the Immortal Construct will attempt to unleash "Scorching Radiance." To stop it, you must break all of its HP shields.

The Immortal Construct will be unable to recover any HP when affected by Stellar Glimmer reactions, no matter what it does...

Located in Everfrozen Earth

Chimeric Winged Lion

◇ A product of bio-alchemy, and one of the flawed artificial monsters cast aside in pursuit of a certain grand ambition.

The Chimeric Winged Lion will oscillate between two enhanced states: "Thundercall" and "Galeforce." When it is in the Thundercall state, it will become weakened when its Elemental Ward is broken. Additionally, any attempts by it to spread its thunder blades can be disrupted by the Stellar Glimmer reaction. When it is in the Galeforce state, it will attempt to absorb any Anemo Orbs that are on the field to charge its attack. At this time, destroying the orbs or using the Stellar Glimmer reaction can stop its charging and weaken it.

Located in Volkodlak Tundra

Within the Polestar Field created by a Starlight Prism, the Eye of Graeae's energy will not deplete and will remain at or above 30%. Prime Ice Constructs inside a Polestar Field will gain various wondrous buffs.

1. If the corresponding area contains a tracked point, including a tracked Teleport Waypoint, the Multi-Layered Map and its button remain visible.

2. Optimizes the display logic when the "Luna Treasure Compass" and "Segment-Domain Treasure Compass" mark clues: Increases the display priority of Oculus icons to prevent them from being obscured by other icons. (The "Cryo Treasure Compass" introduced in Version 7.0 also incorporates this optimization.)
