########## TALENT NAMES ##########


########## EFFECTS DICTIONARY ##########

=== Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling   [Odette:N11500002]
Odette's special Elemental Skill, which deals to nearby opponents <color=#99FFFFFF>AoE Cryo DMG</color> over time. Then, when the dance duet ends, she deals another instance of <color=#99FFFFFF>AoE Cryo DMG</color> that is considered Stellar-Conduct DMG. During the dance duet, Odette can control the direction of the dance steps.\n<color=#FFD780FF>Radiance: Stellar Glimmer</color>: The DMG dealt when the dance duet ends is changed to <color=#99FFFFFF>AoE Cryo DMG</color> of the corresponding Stellar reaction type.\n\nAdditionally, after Odette unleashes the special Elemental Skill <color=#FFD780FF>Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling</color>, should she be in the <color=#FFD780FF>Radiance: Stellar Glimmer</color> state while her <color=#FFD780FF>Dance Double</color> is still on the field, the <color=#FFD780FF>Dance Double</color>'s two dance moves types will also deal additional <color=#99FFFFFF>AoE Cryo DMG</color> of the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction type.

=== Decoding   [Sandrone:N11330001]
Fagio will switch to this mode when using a Charged Attack.\nWhen in this mode, Fagio will engage any opponents ahead with a sweeping fire attack, as well as fire condensed beams at intervals, dealing <color=#99FFFFFF>AoE Cryo DMG</color>.\n<color=#FFD780FF>Radiance: Stellar Glimmer</color>: The condensed beams will instead deal <color=#99FFFFFF>AoE Cryo DMG</color> that is considered the corresponding Stellar Glimmer DMG.

=== Decoding Power   [Sandrone:N11330002]
When in <color=#FFD780FF>Decoding</color> mode, Fagio's <color=#FFD780FF>Decoding Power</color> will continuously increase. When its Decoding Power reaches the maximum, Fagio will be unable to maintain or re-enter Decoding mode.

=== Elevation   [Columbina:N11190007]
Special DMG Boost effect. It is calculated independently of other DMG Boost effects.

=== Frostgrove Sanctuary   [Lauma:N11190001]
A magical array that protects the Moonchanters and can be summoned by using the Elemental Skill <color=#FFD780FF>Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko</color>.\nThe Frostgrove Sanctuary will follow the active character, dealing a single instance of <color=#99FF88FF>AoE Dendro DMG</color> to opponents within the array every 2s. This DMG is considered Elemental Skill DMG.

=== Gravity Ripple   [Columbina:N11250001]
After unleashing Elemental Skill <color=#FFD780FF>Eternal Tides</color>, Columbina will summon <color=#FFD780FF>Gravity Ripple</color>.\nGravity Ripple will follow the current active character on the field, while continuously dealing <color=#80C0FFFF>AoE Hydro DMG</color> to surrounding opponents.\n\nAdditionally, while Gravity Ripple is on the field, and nearby party members trigger Lunar reactions or inflict Lunar Reaction DMG, Columbina will gain the New Moon's Omen effect for 2s, continuously accumulating <color=#FFD780FF>Gravity</color>. 20 Gravity can be accumulated every 2s.\nYou can have up to 60 Gravity, and it will be removed when the duration of Gravity Ripple ends.\nOnce Gravity is accumulated to its limit, a special <color=#FFD780FF>Gravity Interference</color> will be triggered. Different effects will be triggered depending on the Lunar reaction type that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina. If the Lunar reaction type that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina is:\n· <color=#FFD780FF>Lunar-Charged</color>: Gravity Interference will deal a single instance of <color=#FFACFFFF>Electro AoE DMG</color>. This DMG is considered Lunar-Charged DMG. \n· <color=#FFD780FF>Lunar-Bloom</color>: Gravity Interference will fire 5 Moondew Sigils at surrounding opponents, dealing <color=#99FF88FF>Dendro DMG</color>. This DMG is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG.\n· <color=#FFD780FF>Lunar-Crystallize</color>: Gravity Interference will deal a single instance of <color=#FFE699FF>Geo AoE DMG</color>. This DMG is considered Lunar-Crystallize DMG.

=== Lunar Domain   [Columbina:N11250002]
The domain that Columbina summons after unleashing the Elemental Burst <color=#FFD780FF>Moonlit Melancholy</color>.\nWhen current active characters are within the <color=#FFD780FF>Lunar Domain</color>, Lunar Reaction DMG inflicted by all party members will increase.

=== Lunar Phase Shift   [Zibai:N11260001]
After using the Elemental Skill <color=#FFD780FF>Heaven and Earth Made Manifest</color>, Zibai will change to this mode. This mode lasts at most {PARAM#P1263201|4S1}s and has the following properties:\n· Normal and Charged Attacks are converted into <color=#FFE699FF>Geo DMG</color> that cannot be overridden by other infusions.\n· The Elemental Skill <color=#FFD780FF>Heaven and Earth Made Manifest</color> will also be converted to the Elemental Skill <color=#FFD780FF>Spirit Steed's Stride</color>: When Zibai has at least 70 <color=#FFD780FF>Phase Shift Radiance</color>, she can consume 70 Phase Shift Radiance to unleash Spirit Steed's Stride and cause two instances of <color=#FFE699FF>Geo DMG</color>. The second instance will be considered Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG.\n\nA maximum of 100 <color=#FFD780FF>Phase Shift Radiance</color> points can be accrued, and Zibai can accumulate them in the following ways:\n· She gains 10 points every second she is in the <color=#FFD780FF>Lunar Phase Shift</color> mode.\n· She gains 5 points when hitting an opponent with her Normal Attacks. She can gain Phase Shift Radiance points in this way once every 0.5s.\n\n<color=#FFD780FF>Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam</color>\nWhen nearby party members trigger Lunar-Crystallize, they will also accrue 35 <color=#FFD780FF>Phase Shift Radiance</color> points for Zibai. Points can be gained this way once every 4s. Unleashing the special elemental skill <color=#FFD780FF>Spirit Steed's Stride</color> will reset the CD for <color=#FFD780FF>Phase Shift Radiance</color> gained this way.\n\nZibai will leave this mode after unleashing <color=#FFD780FF>Spirit Steed's Stride</color> 4 times, or when the skill's duration ends.

=== Marvelous Splendor   [Odette:N11500003]
After unlocking the Ascension Talent "Spring Rite of the Chosen One," Odette will obtain a special effect when she summons her <color=#FFD780FF>Solo Dance Double</color>.\nEvery stack of <color=#FFD780FF>Marvelous Splendor</color> active increases the character's Stellar Glimmer DMG by 15%. This lasts until her <color=#FFD780FF>Dance Double</color> exits the field or when she summons her <color=#FFD780FF>Solo Dance Double</color> again.\nWhen Odette is off-field, she loses 1 stack of <color=#FFD780FF>Marvelous Splendor</color> every 1 second, while other nearby party members gain the corresponding number of <color=#FFD780FF>Marvelous Splendor</color> stacks at the same time.

=== Moon Song   [Lauma:N11190002]
A melody used to weave together hymns. When an Elemental Burst is used, or for a period of time after an Elemental Burst has been used, it can be converted into Pale Hymn stacks that increase the DMG from Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, and Lunar-Bloom reactions.\nWhen Lauma consumes Verdant Dew to perform the Hold version of her Elemental Skill <color=#FFD780FF>Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko</color>, each Verdant Dew consumed will provide Lauma with 1 stack of Moon Song.

=== Moonridge Dew   [Columbina:N11250003]
A special Verdant Dew resource provided by Columbina's Passive Talent, <color=#FFD780FF>Law of the New Moon</color>. Its maximum capacity and duration are calculated separately from Verdant Dew.\nWhen the party's Verdant Dew is expended, <color=#FFD780FF>Moonridge Dew</color> will be consumed instead.\nYour party can have up to 3 <color=#FFD780FF>Moonridge Dew</color>.

=== Pale Hymn   [Lauma:N11190003]
A hymn passed down by generations of Moonchanters that increases the DMG dealt by Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, and Lunar-Bloom reactions.\n\nLauma can gain Pale Hymn stacks in the following ways:\n· When using her Elemental Burst <color=#FFD780FF>Runo: All Hearts Become the Beating Moon</color>.\n· When consuming Moon Song stacks due to using her Elemental Burst <color=#FFD780FF>Runo: All Hearts Become the Beating Moon</color>.\n· When the Frostgrove Sanctuary triggers the effect of her Constellation <color=#FFD780FF>"I Offer Blood and Tears to the Moonlight"</color>.\n\nWhen party members deal Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, or Lunar-Bloom DMG, 1 stack of Pale Hymn will be consumed and the DMG dealt will be increased based on Lauma's Elemental Mastery. If this DMG hits multiple opponents at once, then multiple stacks of Pale Hymn will be consumed, depending on how many opponents are hit.\nThe duration for each stack of Pale Hymn is counted independently.

=== Phantasm Performance   [Nefer:N11220002]
When in the Shadow Dance state caused by her Elemental Skill <color=#FFD780FF>Senet Strategy: Dance of a Thousand Nights</color>, if you have at least 1 Verdant Dew, Nefer's Charged Attacks will be replaced with the special Charged Attack Phantasm Performance, which will not consume Stamina.\nNefer summons shades of herself to perform Coordinated Attacks against enemies. Nefer and the shades will deal 2 and 3 stages of <color=#99FF88FF>AoE Dendro DMG</color> respectively. DMG dealt by the shades is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG. 1 Verdant Dew will be consumed the first time shades are summoned after every Phantasm Performance.

=== Polestar Field   [Odette:N11330003]
When Stellar-Conduct is triggered, the surrounding area will transform into a <color=#FFD780FF>Polestar Field</color> for a short period of time, and when inside a Polestar Field, certain characters will enter the <color=#FFD780FF>Radiance: Stellar-Conduct</color> state, which grants various skill buffs.

=== Radiance: Stellar Glimmer   [Odette:N11500004]
<color=#FFD780FF>Radiance: Stellar-Conduct</color> and <color=#FFD780FF>Radiance: Stellar Swirl</color> are both considered <color=#FFD780FF>Radiance: Stellar Glimmer</color> states.\nCharacters can only be affected by one Radiance state at any one time, and where more than one state can be triggered, <color=#FFD780FF>Radiance: Stellar-Conduct</color> shall apply first.

=== Shadow Dance   [Nefer:N11220001]
After using the Elemental Skill <color=#FFD780FF>Senet Strategy: Dance of a Thousand Nights</color>, Nefer will enter this state.\nWhile in the Shadow Dance state, if you have at least 1 Verdant Dew, Nefer's Charged Attacks will be replaced with the special Charged Attack Phantasm Performance.\nNefer will exit the Shadow Dance state when she unleashes {PARAM#P1223201|3S1} Phantasm Performance(s) or the state's duration ends.

=== Solo Dance Double   [Odette:N11500001]
A psychic double of Odette's that dances a duet with her. May be summoned to the field through her Elemental Skill or Elemental Burst.\nAlternates between the <color=#FFD780FF>Plume</color> and <color=#FFD780FF>Wing</color> dance moves to periodically attack nearby opponents, dealing <color=#99FFFFFF>AoE Cryo DMG</color>.\nA maximum of one <color=#FFD780FF>Solo Dance Double</color> summoned by Odette is allowed on the field at any one time.\n\nAdditionally, when there is a <color=#FFD780FF>Dance Double</color> summoned by Odette nearby, her Elemental Skill <color=#FFD780FF>Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers</color> is changed to the special Elemental Skill <color=#FFD780FF>Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling</color>.

=== Veil of Falsehood   [Nefer:N11220003]
After absorbing Seeds of Deceit via the Ascension Talent A Wager of Moonlight, Nefer gains this effect, thereby increasing the DMG dealt by her Phantasm Performance.\nEach stack of Veil of Falsehood increases the DMG dealt by Nefer's Phantasm Performance by {PARAM#P1222101|6S100}%. The initial maximum number of stacks is {PARAM#P1222101|1S1}, with each stack lasting for {PARAM#P1222101|2S1}s (each stack's duration is counted independently).

=== Verdant Dew   [Columbina:N11190008]
When party members trigger Lunar-Bloom reactions, the party will receive such a resource.\nYour party will receive 1 Verdant Dew every 2.5s for 2.5s after triggering a Lunar-Bloom reaction. Your party can have up to 3 Verdant Dew. If you trigger another Lunar-Bloom reaction during this time, the duration of this effect will be reset.
